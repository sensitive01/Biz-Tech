1. GLOBAL & CORE FUNCTIONALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Mandatory Billing Module
    • The Billing module is a mandatory core feature and will be active and 
      accessible across all user logins and account tiers.

1.2 Quotation Management for Bulk Orders
    • A dedicated "Quotation" tab/section will be integrated within the sales/billing 
      flow to generate, manage, and track bulk order estimates prior to finalizing invoices.

1.3 Barcode Management Tab
    • A dedicated "Barcode" tab will be dynamically enabled for businesses that operate 
      with both Products and Services, supporting inventory tagging, barcode generation, 
      and scanning.

1.4 Appearance & Accessibility (Dark / Light Mode)
    • Full support for Theme Customization allowing users to seamlessly toggle between 
      Light Mode and Dark Mode based on their preference.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ACCOUNT TIER SPECIFICATIONS & PERMISSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 TYPE 1: SINGLE LOGIN (OWNER-MANAGED)
    • Login Architecture: Single administrative login for the business owner.
    • Feature Scope: Full access to business modules, including Employee Management, 
      Attendance, and Leave Tracking.
    • Operational Model: The business owner records and manages all staff details, 
      daily attendance, and leaves internally; separate employee logins are not required.
    • Capacity Limit: Configurable from Admin login (e.g. 10 employees or custom limit). 
      If nothing is entered / left blank by Admin, there is no limit on employee creation.

2.2 TYPE 2: MULTI-USER (STAFF SELF-SERVICE)
    • Login Architecture: Dedicated multi-user logins for both the business owner and employees.
    • Feature Scope: Mirrors Type 1 features with employee self-service capabilities.
    • Employee Access: Staff members log in to their own portal with access restricted to:
        - Marking / viewing personal Attendance (Check-in / Check-out).
        - Submitting Leave Requests.
    • Leave Approval Workflow: A formal permission/approval system where employee leave 
      requests require owner/manager review and approval.
    • Branch Visibility: Branch management is hidden/omitted for Type 2 accounts as it is 
      intended for single-location businesses.
    • Capacity Limit: Configurable from Admin login. If nothing is entered, unlimited 
      employee creation is allowed. Additional accounts can be expanded as needed.

2.3 TYPE 3: MULTI-BRANCH ENTERPRISE
    • Branch Limit: Unlimited branches can be created under one business organization.
    • Branch Capacity: Each branch is allocated up to 10 employees by default (additional 
      capacity available via add-on tiers).
    • Unique Branch Codes: Each branch will possess a mandatory Branch Code, which is directly 
      mapped to employees stationed at that location.
    • Branch-Based Filtering: Comprehensive branch-level filtering across all modules 
      (dashboards, staff records, sales, transactions, and reports).

2.4 TYPE 3 & TYPE 4: INVESTOR MANAGEMENT & ACCESS
    • Dedicated "Investors" Tab: Available in Type 3 and Type 4 accounts.
    • Creation & Administration: Only the business owner has authority to add/edit investor profiles.
    • Role-Based View Access: Granular read-only view permissions granted to investors, 
      enabling transparent oversight of authorized reports and financial performance.
